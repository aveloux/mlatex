A piece of LaTeX can be set on its own, and the engine runs in the browser.

## Features

- Snippets: `snippet` sets a formula, a table or a picture with no preamble, on a page cropped to it, from C++, C and WebAssembly
- standalone's pages are cropped to what they hold
- The WebAssembly module, `latex.js`, `latex.wasm` and `latex.data`, built and checked in Node by the Build workflow, for a web page, a worker or Node
