One program, `mlatex`, built, tested and packaged for Windows, macOS and Linux.

## Features

- 509 packages and 14 classes, each proven by a document of its own
- Tables that break across pages, with text kept inside its cells
- Right-to-left text: Arabic and Hebrew paragraphs and tables, mirrored brackets, kashida justification and an Arabic face, Noto Naskh Arabic
- Each language's own digits and spacing, and Chinese and Japanese broken between their characters
- More drawing: TikZ's `let` and calc coordinates, trees, nodes with dashes, double outlines and rounded corners, xy-pic, and pgfplots' error bars, fills between plots, functions of x and y and plots in three dimensions
- EPS pictures, drawn by running their PostScript, and SVG pictures
- beamer: each slide of a frame, and beamer's themes
- A `.bib` file imported through `\input`, or set as a document of its own
- An offline switch that keeps a document off the network
- The command line as `mlatex`, with TeX's own options
- Packages for every platform: an MSI, a `.pkg` and a disk image, `.deb` and `.rpm`, and archives, built, tested and packaged on six runners

## Fixes

- The head and foot are placed by `\headsep` and `\footskip`, and keep their marks
- A colour chosen inside a box's argument stays inside it
- A line never ends at a penalty of ten thousand, and a ragged line is filled before it is broken
- A glyph's ink is measured by its outline, not by the box its font file keeps

## Changes

- The engine is `latex` in the code, and the C library's function is `engine()`
- On Windows, any CLion profile builds, with clang-cl chosen for it
- The sandbox nothing ran is removed, and `src/jit` is reserved for the just-in-time target
- A security policy, and a README rewritten around what the engine does today
